# Activity 19 — Qualitative Risk Analysis with GenAI

**Course:** Generative AI for Project Management (C013)  
**Topic 03:** Managing Project Risks with GenAI  
**Learning outcome:** LO3 — Prioritise risks by probability and impact for further action.  
**Tools:** GenAI tool · Probability and Impact Matrix template

## Goal

You cannot act on fifty risks. Qualitative analysis prioritises them so you spend your attention where it matters — and you will test how consistent GenAI's scoring actually is.

## What you'll produce

A scored probability/impact matrix with a prioritised risk shortlist.

## Core prompt

```text
Score each supplied risk for probability and impact from 1 to 5, calculate the risk score, place it in a probability-impact matrix and explain any score that is not directly supported by the data.
```

## Step-by-step

1. Take your risk register from Activity 18. Prompt GenAI: 'Score each risk for probability (1-5) and impact (1-5), calculate the risk score, and place each in a probability/impact matrix.'
2. Run the same prompt a second time in a new chat. Compare the two sets of scores — you will find they differ. Discuss what that inconsistency means for relying on AI scoring.
3. Agree your group's own scores for the top ten risks. Use GenAI's output as a prompt for discussion, not as the answer.
4. Plot the risks on the probability/impact matrix and identify the red zone — high probability, high impact — that needs a response plan.
5. Apply the additional risk factors to your top five: urgency, proximity, dormancy, manageability, controllability, strategic impact, connectivity and propinquity.
6. Prompt: 'For these top five risks, assess proximity (how soon it could occur) and dormancy (how long before we would notice it).' A high-dormancy risk needs a detection mechanism, not just a response.
7. Update your risk register with the agreed scores, the factor assessments and a priority ranking. Share your top three risks with the class.

## Check your work

✅ Your top ten risks have group-agreed scores, your top five carry proximity and dormancy assessments, and you can explain why AI scoring alone is not sufficient.

# Activity 25 — Monitor, Control Change and Close the Project with GenAI

**Course:** Generative AI for Project Management (C013)  
**Topic 03:** Managing Project Risks with GenAI  
**Learning outcome:** LO3 — Control change through the project and close it properly.  
**Tools:** GenAI tool · Digital AI Transformation Roadmap Studio (alfredang.github.io/digitaltransformation) · Change Request template

## Goal

The final activity runs the end of the project lifecycle: processing a change request through integrated change control, then closing the project and capturing lessons learned with GenAI's help.

## What you'll produce

A processed change request with impact analysis, and a project closure report.

## Core prompt

```text
Analyse each change request for its effect on scope, schedule, cost, quality, resources and risk. State confidence in each estimate, recommend an approve, reject or defer decision, and draft the closure actions for approved changes.
```

## Step-by-step

1. In your group, raise a realistic change request against your project — a new regulatory requirement, or a stakeholder asking for an extra feature.
2. Classify it correctly: is it corrective action, preventive action, defect repair, or a change to a baseline? Only the last requires a baseline update.
3. Prompt GenAI: 'Analyse the impact of this change request on scope, schedule, cost, quality and risk. For each, state the impact and how confident you are in the estimate.'
4. Check the impacts it forgot. It will usually cover scope, schedule and cost, and skip the knock-on effects on quality, risk, resources and stakeholder expectations.
5. Run it through integrated change control: raise the request, analyse the impacts, record it in the change log, take it to the Change Control Board for approval/deferral/rejection, and communicate the outcome back to the requester.
6. Now open https://alfredang.github.io/digitaltransformation/ — one member enters their name and clicks 'Create New Roadmap'; the others join with the Roadmap ID or QR code from the Invite button.
7. Add your project's remaining initiatives (title, phase, status, priority, owner, impact 1-10, effort 1-10, start month, duration). Prompt GenAI to propose the impact and effort scores, then adjust them as a group.
8. Read the Impact vs Effort matrix. Identify what falls in Quick Wins versus Thankless, and add your top risks to the Risk Register tab with likelihood, impact and mitigation. Add two or three KPIs with current and target values.
9. Move to closure. Prompt GenAI: 'Draft a project closure report covering: acceptance of deliverables, transition and handover requirements, contract closure, lessons learned on scope changes, schedule impacts, risks and issues, stakeholder and vendor relationships, and recommendations.'
10. Complete the closure checklist: deliverables accepted against acceptance criteria, product transitioned with training and hypercare, contracts and accounts closed, project information archived to organisational process assets, resources released.
11. Use the Export button in the Roadmap Studio to capture your roadmap, and share your closure report with the class.

## Check your work

✅ Your change request is correctly classified with impacts on all five dimensions, it has been through the CCB path, and your closure report covers acceptance, transition, contracts, archiving and resource release.

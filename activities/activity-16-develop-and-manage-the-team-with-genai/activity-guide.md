# Activity 16 — Develop and Manage the Team with GenAI

**Course:** Generative AI for Project Management (C013)  
**Topic 02:** Project Execution and Delivery with GenAI  
**Learning outcome:** LO2 — Advise staff in resolving challenges during project delivery (A2).  
**Tools:** GenAI tool · Claude · Scrum Agile Transformation Studio (alfredang.github.io/scrum)

## Goal

Delivery problems are usually people problems. You will use GenAI to prepare for a difficult conversation and to select a conflict management approach — while judging where AI advice on people is genuinely useful and where it is not.

## What you'll produce

A prepared difficult conversation and a team development plan mapped to Tuckman's stages.

## Core prompt

```text
My project team is in the [stage] stage of Tuckman's model and showing these behaviours [paste]. Recommend leadership actions that can move the team forward, and explain which observations support each action.
```

## Step-by-step

1. In your group, pick a realistic delivery challenge: a team member missing commitments, two members in open conflict, or a team stuck in the storming stage.
2. Locate your team on Tuckman's ladder — Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing or Adjourning — and prompt GenAI: 'My project team is in the [stage] stage and showing [behaviours]. What leadership actions move them to the next stage?'
3. Now prepare a difficult conversation using the Reflection pattern: 'I need to give feedback to a team member who [situation]. They are talented and I want this to help their development. Explain the reasoning and assumptions behind your recommendation for approaching this meeting.'
4. Evaluate the advice critically. It will be reasonable but generic — it knows nothing about this person, your relationship, or your culture. Note precisely which parts you would not use.
5. Review the five conflict management approaches — Smooth/Accommodate, Withdraw/Avoid, Compromise/Reconcile, Force/Direct, Collaborate/Problem Solve — and prompt GenAI to recommend one for your situation with its reasoning.
6. Challenge it: 'When would Force/Direct be the right choice here?' A good PM knows collaboration is not always correct — safety and deadline-critical decisions sometimes require directing.
7. Open https://alfredang.github.io/scrum/ — one member clicks 'Create Workspace', selects a role (Product Owner, Scrum Master, Developer, Stakeholder or Agile Coach) and shares the workspace ID; everyone joins with their own role.
8. Run a retrospective in the studio on your delivery challenge, using GenAI to suggest retrospective prompts. Use the Export panel to download the workspace as CSV or a printable HTML report as your evidence.
9. Discuss as a group: what should a PM never delegate to GenAI when it concerns a specific person?

## Check your work

✅ You have a conversation plan you would actually use, a named conflict approach with justification, an exported retrospective file, and a clear line on what not to delegate to AI.

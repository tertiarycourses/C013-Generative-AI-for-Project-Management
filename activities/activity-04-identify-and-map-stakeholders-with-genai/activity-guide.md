# Activity 4 — Identify and Map Stakeholders with GenAI

**Course:** Generative AI for Project Management (C013)  
**Topic 01:** Project Initiation and Planning with GenAI  
**Learning outcome:** LO1 — Identify stakeholders and plan engagement using GenAI-assisted analysis.  
**Tools:** GenAI tool · Stakeholder Impact Over Influence Matrix.xlsx · Salience Model Template.pptx · Stakeholder Engagement Assessment Matrix.xlsx

## Goal

Projects fail on stakeholders more often than on technology. You will use GenAI to generate a stakeholder register, then map power against interest and decide an engagement strategy for each.

## What you'll produce

A stakeholder register, a power/interest map and a Stakeholder Engagement Assessment Matrix.

## Core prompt

```text
Identify potential stakeholders and their influence and interest levels for a new city-wide bike-sharing system. Explain the evidence needed to validate each rating.
```

## Step-by-step

1. In your group, open 'Stakeholder Impact Over Influence Matrix.xlsx' from the template pack.
2. Prompt GenAI: 'Identify potential stakeholders and their influence/interest levels for a new city-wide bike-sharing system.' — or use your own project.
3. Add the stakeholders GenAI missed. It reliably finds obvious internal roles and misses regulators, unions, adjacent departments and the people who lose something if the project succeeds.
4. Plot each stakeholder on the matrix: X-axis = Influence, Y-axis = Interest. Agree the four quadrant strategies — manage closely, keep satisfied, keep informed, monitor.
5. Now apply the Salience Model. Prompt: 'Assess each stakeholder for power, legitimacy and urgency, and classify them as definitive, dominant, dangerous, dependent, discretionary, demanding or dormant.'
6. Place each stakeholder into the correct salience category in Salience Model Template.pptx and reconcile any disagreement with your power/interest map.
7. Open 'Stakeholder Engagement Assessment Matrix.xlsx'. Prompt GenAI: 'List stakeholders for this project and recommend their current and desired engagement levels (Unaware, Resistant, Neutral, Supportive, Leading) with a matching engagement strategy for each.'
8. Mark C for current and D for desired against each stakeholder, and write the specific action that closes each gap. Share with the class.

## Check your work

✅ Every stakeholder has a quadrant, a salience category, a current and desired engagement level, and one named action to close the gap.

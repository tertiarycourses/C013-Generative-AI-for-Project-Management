# Activity 17 — Plan and Manage Communications with GenAI

**Course:** Generative AI for Project Management (C013)  
**Topic 02:** Project Execution and Delivery with GenAI  
**Learning outcome:** LO2 — Build a communications plan that matches each stakeholder's real needs.  
**Tools:** GenAI tool · your stakeholder register from Activity 4

## Goal

You will calculate how communication complexity grows, then use GenAI to build a communications plan and adapt a single message for very different audiences.

## What you'll produce

A communications management plan and the same update written for three audiences.

## Core prompt

```text
Build a communications management plan from the stakeholder data provided. For each stakeholder specify information needed, purpose, format, level of detail, frequency, channel and sender.
```

## Step-by-step

1. Calculate the communication channels in your project team using N x (N - 1) / 2. For a team of 10 that is 45. Recalculate for 15 people and note how fast it grows.
2. Using your stakeholder register from Activity 4, prompt GenAI: 'Build a communications management plan. For each stakeholder specify: information needed, reason, format, level of detail, frequency, channel, and sender.'
3. Check for the failure mode: the AI will propose sending everything to everyone weekly. Cut it back to what each stakeholder genuinely needs, and use push vs pull deliberately — push for what must be read, pull (a dashboard or portal) for reference information.
4. Now write one piece of bad news — a two-week slip. Prompt GenAI to write it three ways: for the executive sponsor, for the delivery team, and for an external customer.
5. Compare the three against the Five Cs: Correct, Concise, Clear, Coherent, Controlled. Rewrite whichever version fails.
6. Check the sponsor version leads with the impact and the decision required, not with an explanation of what happened.
7. Identify which communication barriers apply in your project — language, cultural, physical, psychological, perceptual, information overload, jargon — and add one mitigation for each to your plan.
8. Share your three versions and explain what you changed between them and why.

## Check your work

✅ Your plan gives each stakeholder a specific frequency and channel, uses push and pull deliberately, and your three message versions genuinely differ in what they lead with.

# Activity 12 — Manage Project Knowledge with GenAI

**Course:** Generative AI for Project Management (C013)  
**Topic 02:** Project Execution and Delivery with GenAI  
**Learning outcome:** LO2 — Capture explicit and tacit knowledge so it is reusable by the organisation.  
**Tools:** GenAI tool · Claude (long-document handling)

## Goal

Knowledge that lives only in people's heads leaves when they do. You will use GenAI to turn messy project conversations into a structured, searchable knowledge asset and a lessons-learned register.

## What you'll produce

A lessons-learned register and a knowledge asset entry for your project.

## Core prompt

```text
From these project records [paste], extract decisions and owners, open questions, lessons learned, and tacit knowledge that is implied but not stated. Quote the evidence used for each extraction.
```

## Step-by-step

1. In your group, write a realistic 'messy input': meeting notes, a chat thread and an email chain about a project problem — roughly a page in total. Invent it if you need to.
2. Prompt GenAI: 'From these project records, extract: (1) decisions made and who made them, (2) open questions, (3) lessons learned, (4) tacit knowledge that is implied but never stated explicitly.'
3. Examine point 4 closely — surfacing tacit knowledge (the 'everyone knows that the vendor is slow in December' type of insight) is where GenAI adds real value here.
4. Build a lessons-learned register entry for each lesson: what happened, the impact, the root cause and the recommendation for future projects.
5. Prompt: 'Rewrite each lesson so it is actionable by a project manager who knows nothing about this project.' Vague lessons are useless lessons.
6. Decide what belongs in the organisational process assets versus what is project-specific, and mark each entry accordingly.
7. Discuss as a group: what are the confidentiality risks of pasting real project records into a public GenAI tool, and what would your organisation's policy allow?

## Check your work

✅ Your register has actionable lessons with root causes, at least one item of genuinely tacit knowledge surfaced, and your group can state your organisation's data-confidentiality limits.

**Remember:** GenAI gives you a fast first draft. Check every figure, source and assumption before the output leaves your hands — you remain accountable for it.

# Activity 3 — Develop the Project Charter with GenAI

**Course:** Generative AI for Project Management (C013)  
**Topic 01:** Project Initiation and Planning with GenAI  
**Learning outcome:** LO1 — Develop project parameters with GenAI according to business requirements.  
**Tools:** GenAI tool · Project Manager Buddy GPT · Project Charter Template.xlsx

## Goal

The charter authorises the project and gives the PM the right to apply resources. You will generate a full charter with GenAI and then correct it against what a charter must actually contain.

## What you'll produce

An approved-quality Project Charter for your group's project.

## Core prompt

```text
Write a project charter for launching an internal knowledge-sharing platform. Include objectives, scope, risks, stakeholders and success criteria. Mark every assumption clearly.
```

## Step-by-step

1. Stay in your group of 3–5 and open 'Project Charter Template.xlsx' from the template pack.
2. Prompt GenAI: 'Write a project charter for launching an internal knowledge-sharing platform. Include objectives, scope, risks, stakeholders, and success criteria.' — or substitute your own project.
3. Check the draft against the required charter contents: sponsor, project manager and key stakeholders named; project description with measurable objectives; business need and financial goals; summary milestone schedule; assumptions, constraints and boundaries; approval requirements and approved budget; success and exit criteria.
4. List every element the AI omitted or left vague. In practice it will usually invent a budget and skip exit criteria.
5. Prompt it again to fix precisely those gaps: 'Revise the charter. Add measurable success criteria, explicit exit criteria, and mark every figure you assumed with [ASSUMPTION].'
6. Replace each [ASSUMPTION] with your group's own figure, then fill in Project Charter Template.xlsx.
7. Use the Persona pattern to stress-test it: 'Act as the project sponsor. Review this charter and list the three questions you would ask before signing it.' Answer those three questions in your charter.
8. Share your charter with the class and state which part GenAI got most wrong.

## Check your work

✅ Your charter names a sponsor and PM, has measurable objectives and explicit success AND exit criteria, and contains no unmarked AI-invented figures.

---

**Remember:** GenAI gives you a fast first draft. Check every figure, source and assumption before the output leaves your hands — you remain accountable for it.

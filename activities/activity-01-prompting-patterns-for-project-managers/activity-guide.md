# Activity 1 — Prompting Patterns for Project Managers

**Course:** Generative AI for Project Management (C013)  
**Topic 01:** Project Initiation and Planning with GenAI  
**Learning outcome:** LO1 — Apply structured prompting patterns to produce usable project management output.  
**Tools:** ChatGPT · Claude · Gemini · Copilot · Project Manager Buddy GPT

## Goal

Work through the ten prompting patterns on a project of your own. You will see the same question answered badly by a vague prompt and well by a structured one, and you will keep a personal prompt library for the rest of the course.

## What you'll produce

A personal prompt library of 10 tested prompts, each with the output it produced and your critique.

## Core prompt

```text
Act as a stakeholder of the project. Perform an assessment of the project charter and provide a list of recommendations to improve scope clarity and completeness.
```

## Step-by-step

1. Open your preferred GenAI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot) and sign in. Open the Project Manager Buddy GPT in a second tab: https://chatgpt.com/g/g-hUcEmp6Rp-project-manager-buddy-project-management
2. Choose a real project from your own workplace — one you know well enough to judge whether the AI output is sensible. Write down its objective, budget range and timeline in one sentence each.
3. Start with a deliberately vague prompt: 'Help me with my project.' Note how generic the answer is. This is your baseline.
4. Now apply the Persona pattern: 'Act as a stakeholder of the project. Perform an assessment of the project charter and provide a list of recommendations to improve scope clarity and completeness.' Compare against the baseline.
5. Apply the Flipped Interaction pattern so the AI interviews you: 'Ask me questions one at a time to prepare for a stakeholder update on [your project].' Answer at least five questions and note which gap it exposed.
6. Apply the Cognitive Verifier pattern to a complex question about your project — one that needs several sub-answers combined. Observe how it decomposes the question.
7. Apply the Fact Check List pattern to a question that depends on external industry data. Verify at least two of the sources it cites actually exist and say what it claims.
8. Record each prompt, the output and a one-line critique in your prompt library. Mark any output that was confidently wrong — this is the habit that protects you all course.

## Check your work

✅ You have 10 prompts in your library, each with output and a critique, and you can state one concrete case where GenAI produced a plausible but wrong answer that you caught.

---

**Remember:** GenAI gives you a fast first draft. Check every figure, source and assumption before the output leaves your hands — you remain accountable for it.

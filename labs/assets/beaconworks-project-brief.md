# BeaconWorks Customer Self-Service Portal — Approved Project Brief

All names and values in this file are synthetic and exist only for the C013 labs.

## Business Need

BeaconWorks Services experiences seasonal increases in customer enquiries. The
support team’s average first-response time reaches 18 hours during peak periods.
Customers repeatedly ask questions already covered by internal guidance, while
agents spend time locating and rewriting the same answers.

## Approved Project Outcome

Deliver a customer self-service portal pilot that lets customers find clear,
accessible answers and lets support agents direct customers to approved content.
The pilot will use the existing website and case-management integration; it will
not replace the case-management platform.

## Objectives

1. Release the approved portal pilot within 12 calendar weeks of project start.
2. Keep total authorised project spending within SGD 120,000.
3. Publish 30 approved English FAQ articles covering the priority enquiry journeys.
4. Demonstrate secure case-reference hand-off, accessibility checks and operational
   support readiness before pilot acceptance.
5. Measure whether average first-response time falls from the 18-hour peak baseline
   toward 10 hours during the three months after launch. This is a future benefit,
   not a condition that can be proven at project close.

## High-Level Deliverables

- Approved requirements and priority customer journeys.
- English content inventory and 30 approved FAQ articles.
- Portal experience and accessibility design.
- Portal configuration in the existing website platform.
- Case-reference integration and non-production test evidence.
- User review, support-agent training, runbook and launch-readiness record.
- Pilot release, support handover and benefit-measurement plan.

## In Scope

- One portal pilot for the Singapore customer-service team.
- English content for the 30 priority FAQ articles.
- Existing website configuration and existing case-management interface.
- Synthetic test data in non-production environments.
- Accessibility review and support-agent workflow preparation.

## Out of Scope

- Replacement of the case-management platform.
- Payment processing, account registration or identity verification.
- Production use of customer records in an AI assistant.
- Languages other than English unless approved through a change record.
- Company-wide rollout beyond the pilot team.

## Constraints and Assumptions

- Budget ceiling: SGD 120,000, including SGD 15,000 management contingency.
- The existing website and interface remain available to the project.
- The support team can provide subject-matter review during agreed windows.
- An external accessibility specialist is needed before acceptance.
- Only synthetic or authorised project data may be entered into an approved AI tool.
- The Service Director is sponsor and acceptance authority.
- The Project Manager owns the integrated project record.

## Initial Milestone Horizon

- Initiation and requirements: weeks 1–2.
- Design and approved content: weeks 2–5.
- Configuration and integration build: weeks 4–8.
- Integration and user review: weeks 8–10.
- Training, launch readiness and pilot: weeks 10–12.

## Initial Risks

- Supplier access to the existing interface may be later than planned.
- Content approval may delay configuration.
- Accessibility specialist availability may conflict with the test window.
- Support agents may not have enough time for design and user review.
- Restricted information could be entered into an unapproved AI service.

## Decision Rights

- Sponsor: approves charter, baseline changes beyond project-manager tolerance,
  pilot acceptance and closure.
- Project Manager: integrates plans, authorises approved work, maintains records,
  escalates forecast breaches and recommends actions.
- Service Operations Lead: owns content accuracy and support readiness.
- IT Integration Lead: owns technical interface evidence.
- Procurement Lead: owns the supplier process and commercial record.
- Data Protection Lead: reviews data handling and approved-tool boundaries.
